Cancellation, Return & Refund Policy

At Velrion Mart, we aim to provide a reliable and straightforward shopping experience. Please read this policy carefully before placing an order.

1. Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of an order before the order has been processed or dispatched.

Once an order has been processed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible.

To request a cancellation, please contact our support team as soon as possible with your Order ID.

2. Return Eligibility

A return or replacement may be considered when a product is received in a damaged, defective, incorrect or materially different condition from the product ordered.

Products must be returned in an acceptable condition and, where applicable, with their original packaging, accessories and other included items.

Return eligibility may vary depending on the product and its condition.

3. Items Not Eligible for Return

Returns may not be accepted in cases involving:

  • Products damaged after delivery due to misuse or improper handling.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Products returned without required accessories or included items.
  • Products that have been altered, used improperly or intentionally damaged.
  • Products specifically identified as non-returnable on the product page.

4. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, contact us promptly with your Order ID and clear photographs or other relevant information about the issue.

Our team will review the request and determine the appropriate resolution, which may include replacement, return or refund, depending on the circumstances and product eligibility.

5. Refunds

When a refund is approved, the applicable refund amount will be processed to the original payment method or through another suitable method communicated by Velrion Mart.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or financial institution.

6. Return Shipping

Where a return is approved because the product was damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied, Velrion Mart may provide further instructions regarding the return process.

For other return requests, any applicable return shipping conditions will be communicated during the return review.

7. How to Request a Return or Refund

Please contact our support team with the following information:

  • Order ID
  • Product name
  • Reason for the request
  • Relevant photographs or supporting information, where applicable

Our team will review the request and guide you through the next steps.

8. Policy Updates

Velrion Mart may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, products or operational requirements. The updated version will be published on this page.
